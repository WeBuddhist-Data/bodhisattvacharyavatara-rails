## Today's Verse

> Therefore, those who are present
> to destroy my praise and such—
> do I not engage with them to protect myself
> from falling into the lower realms? ^6-99

> I seek liberation,
> so I do not need to be bound by gains and respect.
> How can I be angry at those
> who free me from my bondage? ^6-100

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the ninety-ninth and hundredth verses of the sixth chapter. In your life, who frees you from bondage without flattering you?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. Praise and such things are the basis of all faults. Enemies who destroy that basis protect us from falling into the lower realms. Someone who seeks liberation does not need the gains and respect that bind people to samsara. Enemies free us from that bondage, so it is not right to be angry at them.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, if other people criticize me, I will not get angry, and I will think that they are very kind to me.

**Explanation:** _(Patience Practice)_ This helps us become free from the bondage of gains and respect. When we give up hatred, the mind gains peace. This is the best path for those who seek liberation.
