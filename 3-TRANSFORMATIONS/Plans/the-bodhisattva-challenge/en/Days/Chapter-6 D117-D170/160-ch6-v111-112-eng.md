## Today's Verse

> Therefore, because patience arises
> by relying on intense hatred,
> that very hatred is the cause of patience,
> and is therefore worthy of offering, like the sacred teachings. ^6-111

> Therefore, the Sage declared that sentient beings
> are a field of merit, and the Buddhas are a field of merit.
> By pleasing these, many
> have thus perfected transcendent qualities. ^6-112

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the one hundred and eleventh and one hundred and twelfth verses of the sixth chapter. If you treat the next person you meet today like a field for planting precious seeds, what will grow?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following quotation, which is easy to understand and encouraging. The _Dharmasangiti Sutra_ says, "The field of sentient beings is the field of the buddhas. From that field, the qualities of a buddha are gained. It is not right to act wrongly toward it." This means that sentient beings are the field where we gain the qualities of a buddha. So we must not act wrongly toward them or harm them.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will do one small helpful action for a person and make them happy.

**Explanation:** _(Doing good)_ If we please sentient beings, our store of merit becomes complete. Through this, we reach the path of the perfections. Sentient beings are a field of merit, just like the buddhas.
