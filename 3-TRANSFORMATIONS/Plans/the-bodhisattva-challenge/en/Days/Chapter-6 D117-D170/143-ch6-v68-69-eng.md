## Today's Verse

> Why did one previously commit
> the actions that cause others to be harmed?
> If everything depends on karma,
> why do I blame this? ^6-68

> Seeing this, I will certainly
> strive to perform good deeds
> so that all beings
> mutually develop loving-kindness. ^6-69

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the sixty-eighth and sixty-ninth verses of the sixth chapter. Is the person who harms you today only paying back the result of karma that you made yourself in the past?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. The harm that others do to us now is the result of karma ripening. In the past, we harmed others. So it is not right to be angry at the one who harms us. All our happiness and suffering depend on earlier good and bad karma. Beings, under the power of karma and the afflictions, harm one another and cannot stop. So with compassion we must gather merit, and lead all, enemies and loved ones, to love one another like parents and children.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, if others find fault with me, I will think that it is my own karma, and I will not get angry.

**Explanation:** _(Patience Practice)_ This purifies bad karma from earlier. By not blaming others, peace arises in the mind. This is the power of patience.
