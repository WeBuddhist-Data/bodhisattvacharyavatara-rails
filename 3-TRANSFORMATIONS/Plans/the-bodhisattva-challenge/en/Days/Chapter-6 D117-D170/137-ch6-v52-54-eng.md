## Today's Verse

> The mind is not physical,
> so no one can destroy it anywhere.
> Because of clinging to the body,
> the body is harmed by sufferings. ^6-52

> If contempt, harsh words,
> and unpleasant speech
> do not harm the body,
> why, mind, are you so angry? ^6-53

> If others' dislike of me
> does not consume me in this life
> or the next,
> why do I dislike it? ^6-54

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the fifty-second, fifty-third, and fifty-fourth verses of the sixth chapter. If bad words do not make a wound on our body, why does such a dart arise in our mind when someone speaks unkind words?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. The mind has no form, so weapons and sticks cannot destroy it. The mind takes the body as its own and clings to it. Because of this, the suffering of beating and hitting harms the body. Contempt and harsh words do not harm the body even a little. If these bad words do not harm the body, it goes without saying that they do not harm the mind. So it is not right to be angry at them.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, even if others say harsh words, I will think that they do not harm my body, and I will give up anger.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ Harsh words cannot cause even a little harm to our body. If we think like this, anger does not arise and the mind stays at peace. This frees us from the suffering of the mind.
