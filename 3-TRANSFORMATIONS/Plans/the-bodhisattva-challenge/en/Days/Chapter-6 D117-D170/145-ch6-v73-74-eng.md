## Today's Verse

> If I cannot endure
> even this much present suffering,
> why do I not avert the anger
> that is the cause of hellish suffering? ^6-73

> For the sake of desires, I have experienced
> thousands of burnings and such in hell,
> but I have accomplished neither my own benefit
> nor the benefit of others. ^6-74

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the seventy-third and seventy-fourth verses of the sixth chapter. If we cannot bear even a needle prick or a small bad word, how will we face the suffering that anger creates?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. We cannot bear even a small suffering, such as being pinched with fingernails, hit with a whip, or stung by a bee. Hatred is the cause of being born in hell and feeling the suffering of heat and cold. So those who are afraid of suffering must surely turn back this hatred. Under the power of hatred, people have felt hell-like suffering many thousands of times to get what they want. Even so, neither their own good nor the good of others is achieved.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when suffering and bad words reach my body and mind, I will turn back anger and meditate on patience.

**Explanation:** _(Patience Practice)_ This protects us from the suffering of hell in the next life. By bearing a small suffering now, we block the bad cause of anger. Peace and happiness arise in the mind.
