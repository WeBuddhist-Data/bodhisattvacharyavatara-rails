## Today's Verse

> Since my master himself
> does not care for his own body,
> how can I, foolish as I am,
> be arrogant and not act as a servant? ^6-121

> The Buddhas rejoice in happiness,
> and are displeased when harm is done.
> They are all pleased by their joy,
> and harming them harms the Buddhas. ^6-122

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the one hundred and twenty-first and one hundred and twenty-second verses of the sixth chapter. Today, toward which person around you are you proud, seeing them as lower than yourself?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. Sentient beings are like the beloved children of the buddhas. When sentient beings are glad and happy, all the buddhas become pleased. If harm comes to sentient beings, the buddhas become displeased. So if we harm sentient beings, we harm the buddhas. For the sake of sentient beings, the buddhas did not even care for their own bodies. So we who are ignorant must not be proud toward sentient beings. We must do the work of a servant.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will not be proud toward others, and I will take a humble place and do one act of service.

**Explanation:** _(Doing good)_ This destroys our own pride. It helps others, and the mind becomes calm. This is the best way to learn by following the Buddha.
