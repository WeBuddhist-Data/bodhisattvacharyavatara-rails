## Today's Verse

> If I am happy because of their happiness,
> then I should do the same for everyone.
> How can I be unhappy
> if I am happy for others? ^6-96

> Therefore, that my own joy arises
> because I am praised
> is also inappropriate,
> and is merely childish behavior. ^6-97

> Praise and such distract me.
> They also destroy weariness.
> They cause envy towards those with good qualities,
> and they destroy prosperity. ^6-98

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the ninety-sixth, ninety-seventh, and ninety-eighth verses of the sixth chapter. After other people have praised you, where has your mind gone?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. Praise and fame distract us from doing good. Because of them, we no longer feel weary of the suffering of samsara. They make jealousy arise toward the good qualities of others. Besides this, they destroy our merit and our roots of goodness.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when others praise me, I will notice that my mind is distracted, and I will focus my mind on my work.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ If we are attached to praise, our good mind gets weaker. If we practice like this, we give up jealousy and distraction. This helps to tame our own mind.
