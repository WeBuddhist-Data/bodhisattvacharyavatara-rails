## Today's Verse

> Let alone the future buddhahood
> that arises from pleasing sentient beings,
> have you not seen great glory,
> fame, and happiness in this very life? ^6-133

> In cyclic existence, through patience, one obtains
> beauty and such, freedom from illness, and fame.
> One lives for a very long time,
> and attains the great happiness of a universal monarch. ^6-134

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the one hundred and thirty-third and one hundred and thirty-fourth verses of the sixth chapter. Tomorrow, when you get up in the morning, which of the gifts that patience brings, such as a beautiful body, good health, and a long life, would you most like to receive?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. By pleasing sentient beings, we will reach the state of a perfect buddha in the future. In this life too, we gain wealth, a good name, and happiness. If we meditate on patience in samsara, we gain a beautiful and healthy body, fame, a long life, and the happiness of a universal monarch.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when hatred arises, I will stay quiet and meditate on patience.

**Explanation:** _(Patience Practice)_ If we rely on patience, the mind becomes happy and the body becomes healthy. It makes life long and fame grow. This is the cause of gaining happiness in samsara.
