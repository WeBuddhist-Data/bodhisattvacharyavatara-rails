## Today's Verse

> Furthermore, if you do not desire
> this happiness, thinking, "They will also be happy,"
> because you abandon giving rewards and such,
> both visible and invisible good deeds will be ruined. ^6-78

> When speaking of one's own good qualities,
> one desires happiness for others.
> When speaking of others' good qualities,
> one does not desire happiness for oneself. ^6-79

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the seventy-eighth and seventy-ninth verses of the sixth chapter. When was the last time you praised the good qualities of another person in a clear voice?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. Suppose we praise others and they become glad, but we are not glad about it. Then we would not be allowed to give wages or help even to servants and workers. Why? Because they too become glad when they receive wages. If we act like this, we will lose the benefit of this life and later lives. The happiness of others is a cause of our own happiness. So being jealous goes against the conduct of someone with bodhicitta.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will praise one good quality of another person.

**Explanation:** _(Doing good)_ This removes our jealousy. It brings joy in the good qualities of others. This is a good action.
