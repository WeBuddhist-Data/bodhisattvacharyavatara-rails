## Today's Verse

> Someone who experiences happiness for a hundred years in a dream
> and then awakens,
> and someone else who experiences happiness for a moment
> and then awakens— ^6-57

> for both of them, upon awakening,
> that happiness is gone.
> Both a long life and a short life
> are merely like that at the time of death. ^6-58

> Even if one obtains many gains
> and enjoys happiness for a long time,
> like someone whose possessions are stripped by robbers,
> one goes away naked and empty-handed. ^6-59

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the fifty-seventh, fifty-eighth, and fifty-ninth verses of the sixth chapter. When this life ends, what can we really take with us?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable example, which is easy to understand and encouraging. For example, a thief takes wealth from its owner. In the same way, at the time of death, however much food, clothing, and relatives and friends we have, we have no power to take even one with us. People must go alone. We must go to the world beyond this one without clothes and with empty hands. Gains and other things like these do not last, and they have no essence.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will not cling to my own wealth, and I will give food to others.

**Explanation:** _(Generosity Practice)_ At the time of death, we must leave behind all food and wealth and go with empty hands. If we give to others today, we gather a great store of merit. This is the best way to take the essence from wealth that does not last.
