## Today's Verse

> If you do not desire this for beings,
> what is that awakening you desire?
> If you are angry at others' prosperity,
> how can you have the Mind of Awakening? ^6-83

> If they receive it from that,
> or if the patron remains in the house,
> if it is not for you at all,
> what does it matter whether it is given or not? ^6-84

> Why do you abandon your good deeds and faith,
> or your own good qualities?
> Tell me, who would not be angry
> at not holding onto what has been gained? ^6-85

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the eighty-third, eighty-fourth, and eighty-fifth verses of the sixth chapter. When a colleague receives a reward, if we feel jealous, what good qualities of our own do we lose?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. Whether a giver gives things to our enemy or does not give them, those things do not become ours. So it is pointless to be angry that another person gets gains. Even if we get angry, we gain nothing for ourselves.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, when another person receives praise, I will not be jealous, and I will meditate on joy.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ If we feel joy at the wealth of others, it overcomes jealousy. This helps bodhicitta arise in our mind. This is the real practice of a bodhisattva.
