## Today's Verse

> This is not so harmful,
> and great purposes will be accomplished.
> Therefore, it is appropriate to only rejoice
> in the suffering that removes the harm of beings. ^6-75

> If, when others praise those with good qualities,
> you obtain the happiness of joy,
> why do you not rejoice in the same way
> by praising them, mind? ^6-76

> This happiness of your joy
> is blameless and a source of happiness.
> It is permitted by those with good qualities,
> and it is also the supreme way to attract others. ^6-77

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the seventy-fifth, seventy-sixth, and seventy-seventh verses of the sixth chapter. Today, when we hear another person being praised, what kind of feeling will come if we too feel glad?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. When we praise the good qualities of others, meditating on joy is a happiness that becomes a cause of ultimate happiness. There is no fault in this. The buddhas and bodhisattvas, who have all good qualities, never stop it. They allow it. This shows that we are free of jealousy, so it makes others have faith in us. So this is the best way to draw others close.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will praise the good qualities of a person I do not like, and I will meditate on joy.

**Explanation:** _(Doing good)_ If we praise the good qualities of others, it stops jealous thoughts. Joy and peace arise in our own mind. This is the best good action, one that helps both ourselves and others.
