## Today's Verse

> If I understand my own purpose,
> what purpose is there in that?
> If I only desire mental ease,
> I should also rely on ornaments and alcohol. ^6-91

> If one loses wealth for the sake of fame
> and even kills oneself,
> what is the point of mere words?
> To whom is that flesh good? ^6-92

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the ninety-first and ninety-second verses of the sixth chapter. When we give even our wealth and our life for the empty praise of others, what price are we really paying?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. Some ignorant people throw away their wealth like grass for the sake of fame. They want to be called heroes, so they go to the battlefield and even give up their own lives. Praise and fame are only words with no essence, so these people lose even the happiness of this life. Such happiness is only what children enjoy, so the wise do not rely on it.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will give up actions done to get the praise of others, and I will tame my mind.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ Words of praise are empty sounds, so they bring no benefit. If we are not attached to fame, the mind stays peaceful. So this is the best way to tame our own mind.
