## Today's Verse

> If a sandcastle collapses,
> children cry out in distress.
> Likewise, if praise and fame are lost,
> my mind is like a child's. ^6-93

> For the time being, sound is inanimate,
> so there cannot be a mind that praises me.
> That it is known that others are glad for me—
> is that a cause for gladness? ^6-94

> Whether for others or for myself,
> what benefit is there for me in others' happiness?
> That joy and happiness belong to them.
> I do not obtain a fraction of it. ^6-95

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the ninety-third, ninety-fourth, and ninety-fifth verses of the sixth chapter. What is the sandcastle that you are guarding today?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following example, which is easy to understand and encouraging. Children build sandcastles. When the castle falls down, they cry loudly. In the same way, if we grieve when we lose praise and fame, our mind is like a child's mind, and it has no real purpose. The sound of praise has no mind, so it has no wish to praise us. The joy others feel in praising us belongs to them. We do not get even a little of it.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, even if other people criticize me, I will not get angry like a child, and I will meditate on patience.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ Like a sandcastle, praise and fame do not last forever. If we meditate like this, our mind gains peace and happiness. We become free from the child-like suffering of hopes and fears.
