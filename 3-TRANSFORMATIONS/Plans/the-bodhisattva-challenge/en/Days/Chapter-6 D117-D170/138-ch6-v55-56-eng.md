## Today's Verse

> If I dislike this
> because it hinders my gains,
> I will abandon my gains here,
> but my misdeeds will remain firm. ^6-55

> It is better for me to die right now
> than to live long by wrong livelihood.
> Even if I live long,
> the suffering of death is the same. ^6-56

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the fifty-fifth and fifty-sixth verses of the sixth chapter. When others get in the way of our profit and our livelihood, we think together about this: in the end, all of us come to the same place of death.

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following valuable quotation, which is easy to understand and encouraging. This quotation comes from the _Smrityupasthana_ sutra. "Whoever lives by wrong livelihood, even only a little, sinks into filth, and worms eat them." This shows that the fault of wrong livelihood has no limit. It is taught that it is better to die today than to live a long time by a wrong livelihood that harms others. However long we live, there is no way to avoid feeling the suffering of death.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, I will not flatter people in order to get gains. I will stay honest.

**Explanation:** _(Avoiding wrongdoing)_ A wrong livelihood does not bring benefit to ourselves or to others. If we live honestly, we will have no regret at the time of death. This is a life with meaning, even if it is short.
