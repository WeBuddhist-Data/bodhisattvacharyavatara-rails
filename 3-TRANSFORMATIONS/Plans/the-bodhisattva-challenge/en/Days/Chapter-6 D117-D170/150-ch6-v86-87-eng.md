## Today's Verse

> and are not free from sorrow.
> Do you also want to compete
> with others who have performed good deeds?
> End of Section Two. ^6-86

> If an enemy is unhappy,
> what is there for you to be glad about?
> Your mere wish
> will not cause harm to them. ^6-87

## 1) Introduction to Today's Practice

Today's practice is based on the eighty-sixth and eighty-seventh verses of the sixth chapter. Have you ever made anything really happen by hoping that something bad would happen to another person?

## 2) Commentary Explanation

The commentaries on these verses give us the following point, which is easy to understand and encouraging. It is not right to be unhappy when others get gains and praise and we do not. In the past, we did faults such as taking what was not given, and we did not regret it. So we made the cause of not getting gains. On top of that, to want to compete with those who gathered great merit as the cause of perfect wealth is a laughing matter.

## 3) Today's Practice

**Actual Practice:** Today, if I see hardship come to others and a thought of gladness arises in me, I will give it up at once.

**Explanation:** _(Taming the mind)_ Being glad at the hardship of others cannot harm them. It brings the afflictions into our own mind. If we give up the bad thought, peace and happiness arise in our own mind.
